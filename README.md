# 📚 UGV Lecture Sheet Rater

A Chrome extension that rates all university lecture sheets with ONE click. No more wasting time rating each sheet individually!

---

## 🎯 About The Project

As a student of the **Department of Computer Science and Engineering** at **University of Global Village (UGV)**, I know the struggle of preparing for exams. Every semester, we have to rate each lecture sheet individually just to download and study. This repetitive task takes up valuable time that could be spent on actual studying.

**This extension solves that problem.**

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ Rate ALL lecture sheets at once with one click
- ✅ Auto-submit ratings - no manual submission needed
- ✅ Simple and intuitive interface
- ✅ University branded design (#11458A)
- ✅ Saves 15-30 minutes per session
- ✅ Works seamlessly with the UGV portal

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Open your UGV lecture sheet page
2. Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar
3. Choose your rating:
   - 🤮 Very Bad
   - 🤢 Bad
   - 😐 Average
   - 🙂 Good
   - 🫡 Very Good
   - 👏😱 Excellent
4. Click "Rate All"
5. Done! All sheets rated instantly 🎉
